PIN Letter Sorter

Match HSC minimum standards PIN letters to a class roll, then save that class's letters as one printable PDF, in roll order.

Runs on this computer only. Nothing is uploaded.

1PIN letter PDFs

Select all three PDFs at once (Ctrl/Cmd-click).

    2Class roll

    Paste names only, one per line. A NESA student number on the line (if the letters show it) gives the most reliable match. Remove class codes and other columns.

    3Minimum standards cross check (optional)

    The NESA results CSV with NESA Student Id, Family Name, Given Name/s and the READING, WRITING and NUMERACY Minimum Standard Met columns. Each student is checked against it: who still needs which online test, who needs a test but has no letter, and who has a letter but has already met all three. Roll names without a NESA number pick up their number from the CSV for a more reliable letter match.

    4Match and save

    Load the PDFs and paste the roll to begin.
